How to Play

The Goal

Reduce the enemy's HP to zero before they do the same to you. Each player starts with 30 HP.

The Board

Six lanes. Each holds one of your cards in the bottom slot and one enemy card in the top slot. Empty lanes let cards attack the HP bar directly.

Energy & Turns

You spend Energy to play cards. You gain Energy equal to the round number each round — round 1 gives 1, round 2 gives 2, etc. Most matches end around round 6, but Energy keeps scaling if a fight goes longer.

Each round has three phases:

  • Cards — play creatures into lanes.
  • Tricks — play instant effects (damage, control, swing tools).
  • Combat — every lane attacks simultaneously.
Cards
  • Common — cost 1-3
  • Uncommon — cost 4-6
  • Rare — cost 7-8
  • Legendary — cost 9-10

Each card shows its Attack (green orb) and Health (red orb). Keywords like Evade, Armor, Splash, Bullseye, Overdrive, Taunt, Freeze appear as chips under the name — hover them in-game for a tooltip.

Block Meter

When you take damage, your Block Meter fills. At 8/8 the meter triggers during the next Tricks phase: a free Trick is added to your hand and the next incoming damage is fully absorbed. The meter resets when consumed.

Tricks

Small purple cards below your hand. Play them during the Tricks phase for one-shot effects: damage, heal, buff, control.

Tricks are single-use. Once played, they leave your deck permanently — they don't go to the dead pile, and they can't be revived. Plan your trick draft accordingly: each trick you take is one swing tool you'll have ALL run, but only ONCE.

Draft Strategy

Classic mode starts with a 5-round card draft and 2-round trick draft. You see two options each round — pick the stronger card OR the one that combos with what you've already taken.

  • Curve out: aim for at least one playable card at cost 1-2-3-4 so you're never stuck.
  • One big threat: a 9 or 10-cost card (Magneto, Trigon, Hulk, Darth Vader, Darkseid) can swing the late game — worth a pick.
  • Combo hunters: Hela pairs with cheap fodder; Cyborg pairs with anything in hand; Dr. Strange peeks the draw pile.
  • Each draft phase gives one Mulligan — click the button to re-draw the current pair. Use it when both picks are weak.
Chain Attacks

Some cards chain their damage across consecutive enemies:

  • Wonder Woman's Lasso — her chain hits the enemy in her lane + one lane left/right, reducing damage by 1 per step.
  • Captain America's Shield — bounces for 3, 2, 1 down the line if the lane's main hit lands.
  • Darth Vader's Choke — 7 damage to any enemy, then 6, 5, 4, … in a direction you pick.
  • Trigon — chain-freezes consecutive enemies when played.

If Armor fully absorbs a chain hit, the chain stops there. Plan around it — a 2-Armor wall blocks low-damage chains entirely.

Status Effects

Cards can take on temporary states that change how they behave:

  • Mind Control (Gorilla Grodd, Luke Skywalker, Mind Stone, Michael Myers) — the controlled card swings at one of its own allies next combat. You pick which.
  • Fear (Joker, Batman, Fear Toxin, Darth Vader) — the target attacks itself next combat, often killing it outright.
  • Freeze — can't attack, dodge, move, or make bonus attacks; Palpatine doubles his damage to frozen targets.
  • Crazy — Joker stamps "Crazy" on the highest-ATK enemy. Crazy cards reroll their ATK between 1-4 every turn (chaotic).
  • Insane — Joker's intrinsic state. He rolls ATK 2-7 every turn — Fear and Steady can't suppress it.
  • Charmed — Poison Ivy charms a random ally. The charmed card's ATK is "loaned" to Ivy for the round; the charmed card swings normally on its own lane.

Etches let you mitigate or apply status. Steady (rare etch) cancels Crazy rerolls per charge. Immunity negates incoming debuffs.

Jump Mechanics

Some cards have Jump conditions that let them enter the board FOR FREE when a specific trigger fires:

  • Ghostface jumps in after an enemy trick fires.
  • Michael Myers jumps to lock onto the next enemy ≤ cost 4 played.
  • Jason Voorhees jumps into an open lane when an ally dies.

When your jump trigger fires, a popup asks which lane you want the card to enter — declining keeps the card in hand for later.

Advanced Defense
  • Evade N — dodges the next N attacks (consumes one charge per evade).
  • Armor N — subtracts N from every incoming damage instance.
  • Invincible N — negates all damage for N turns.
  • Immunity N — negates N incoming debuffs (Freeze/Fear/Mind Control).
  • Untrickable — automatic on every 10-cost card; tricks simply don't affect them.
  • Unresistible — bypasses one Immunity charge; consumed when it fires.
Roguelite Mode

A separate mode from the main menu. Climb a 3-act ladder of fights, each with a boss at the end:

  • Run map — choose your path through combat, elite, treasure, rest, shop, and event nodes. Each act ends with a boss.
  • Deck evolves — start with a small starter deck. Earn cards from victory rewards, level them up via XP, and apply Etches that scale their abilities.
  • Etches — keyword upgrades cards earn on level-up: stat bumps (+1 ATK, +1 HP, +1/+1), traits (Bullseye, Splash, Evade, Hunt, Untrickable, Armor, Taunt, Cantrip, Echo, Phoenix), energy (Discount), and Text+ (rewrites the card's text with stronger numbers).
  • Relics — passive run-wide modifiers earned from bosses, treasure chests, and events. Each one tilts the run a different way.
  • Curses — penalty cards that join your deck from risky events. They clog your hand and may have downsides when played. Removable at Rest Sites.
  • Boss intro splash — when you click a boss node, a cinematic banner shows the boss persona, archetype, and signature cards before the fight starts.

Energy in Roguelite scales 2× faster than Classic — round 1 starts at 2 energy. Combats end faster, so each play matters more.

Tips
  • Click the deck count in the HUD to see what's left in your draw pile.
  • Click a card in your hand, then click a lane to play it.
  • Long-press any card on phone/tablet to see its full description in a modal.
  • Hit ? anywhere to open the keyboard shortcuts + keyword cheat sheet.
  • The active lane pulses gold during combat.
  • Your Neon Theme (Settings) sets the colour of your side of the board.
  • Settings → Colorblind mode adds stripes to enemy cards if you need it.
  • Settings → Export Backup backs up your saved decks + preferences as JSON.

Turn structure

  1. Phase 1 — first player plays cards
  2. Phase 2 — second player plays cards & tricks
  3. Phase 3 — first player plays tricks
  4. Combat — lanes resolve left → right
  5. Draw + new round (+1 energy)

Keywords

  • Evade — dodges one attack; charge-based
  • Armor N — reduces incoming damage by N
  • Splash N — hits adjacent lanes for N
  • Bullseye — bypasses block meter
  • Overdrive — extra swing if lane is open
  • Taunt — forces attackers to target this card
  • Immunity — ignores N debuffs / kills
  • Invincible N — absorbs all damage for N turns
  • Freeze N — can't attack, move, or bonus-attack for N turns
  • Unresistible — bypasses one Immunity charge
  • Untrickable — immune to tricks (all 10-cost cards)
